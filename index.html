How It Works

1. Access from Menu Bar

Click the Stretch It icon in your menu bar—it's always there, ready when you are.

2. Drag to Stretch

Pull down with your cursor. Distance controls duration: short for quick breaks, long for focused sessions.

3. Modify with Keys

OPTION for seconds. SHIFT for extra time. Release to start—your timer begins immediately.

4. Stay Productive

Visual feedback in the menu bar. Notifications alert you when time's up, keeping you in flow.
